# Buildr101 — promotion kit (2026-07-16)

Ready-to-use launch copy. Everything below is honest to the shipped product: plain-English
builds, real accounts/data, real photography, live publishing, 30 free credits no card,
from £12/mo, full code export. Don't promise anything not in that list.

## Positioning

**One-liner:** Describe an app in plain English. Buildr101 builds it, designs it with real
photography, and puts it live on a URL — in minutes, from £12/month.

**Audiences, in priority order:**
1. **Small business owners** (barbers, salons, cafés, trades, studios) — they want a *site
   that does something* (bookings, enquiries) without paying an agency £500+.
2. **Indie makers / side-hustlers** — fastest way from idea to working MVP with accounts + data.
3. **No-code crowd** — Lovable/Base44 users who want cheaper, simpler, UK-priced.

**Price anchor to use everywhere:** "A web agency charges £500+ and takes three weeks.
This takes one sentence and three minutes. From £12/month, first builds free."

## THE asset: the 90-second demo video (make this first — everything links to it)

Screen-record the real product, no faking, one take is fine:

1. **(0:00–0:08)** Blank builder. Type: *"a website for a local barber shop: hero, services
   with prices, opening hours, about the shop, and a booking request form"* — hit Generate.
2. **(0:08–0:50)** The build timeline doing its thing (speed up 2–4× in editing). Voiceover or
   captions: "It's writing real code. Real photos. A working booking form — not a mockup."
3. **(0:50–1:10)** The preview appears. Scroll the finished site slowly — hero photo, prices,
   hours, form. Submit a booking to show it actually works.
4. **(1:10–1:25)** One iteration: type *"make the theme darker and add a gift cards section"*
   → show the change landing.
5. **(1:25–1:30)** Click Publish → the live URL in a fresh tab. End card: **buildr101.com —
   30 free credits, no card needed.**

Cuts: 30s vertical version for TikTok/Reels/Shorts (steps 1, 3, 5 only). The build timelapse
alone is inherently watchable — the typing prompt + files appearing is the hook.

## Reddit (value-first framing — these communities nuke ads)

**r/SideProject / r/indiehackers — "I built" post:**
Title: *I built an AI app builder that takes a plain-English sentence to a published web app —
here's the barber-shop site it built from one prompt*
Body: your honest builder story (solo, self-hosted on a VPS, Stripe/Supabase stack, why you
built it vs Lovable/Base44), the demo video, and the prompt + the result side by side. End
with: free tier, 30 credits, would love feedback. Answer every comment.

**r/smallbusiness / r/sweatystartup — value-first (do NOT lead with your product):**
Title: *You probably don't need to pay £500+ for a basic business website in 2026*
Body: honest rundown of the current options (Wix/Squarespace = templates + monthly fees,
agencies = £££, AI builders = new third option), what AI builders can and can't do, mention
yours as the one you make (with disclosure), link the demo. The disclosure + genuine
comparison is what keeps it up.

## Claims discipline

Never say: "unlimited", "free forever", "no coding knowledge could ever be needed", "custom
domains on all plans" (Pro+ only), or show a build that was manually touched up. The landing
page's "Built from that sentence. Untouched." standard applies to every asset.
