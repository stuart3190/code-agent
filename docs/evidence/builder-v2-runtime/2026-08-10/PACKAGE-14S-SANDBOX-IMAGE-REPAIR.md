`shell/server/lib/builderV2/sandboxProvenance.mjs` is the single definition of which files decide
a verdict. The image bakes its identity at build time; the sandbox answers a `sandbox_provenance`
job with a live recomputation plus the baked record; `runtimeComposition` compares host and
sandbox **before the orchestrator's first model call** and throws `sandbox_version_mismatch`,
naming the differing files, at zero credits.
